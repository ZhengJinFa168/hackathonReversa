Do lobby comments show up in the AI Act?

Stakeholder comments on the AI Act (Have Your Say 2020 and 2021) matched against the Commission proposal (Track A) and the European Parliament committee amendments (Track B), followed through the EP report to Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. An echo is a text match above a null-calibrated threshold, judged by an LLM; it is evidence of similar wording, not proof of cause.
